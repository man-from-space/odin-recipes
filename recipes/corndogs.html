Corn Dogs

Delicious home made corn dog recipe that you can make at home.

This is a recipe for corn dogs that I haven't tried. It could be good, or not. I don't know. Make it if you want too. You do want to don't you?

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cornmeal
  • 1 cup all-purpouse flour
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 4 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon pepper
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 quart vegetable oil
  • 2 (16 ounce) packs of hot dogs
  • 16 wooden skewers

Directions

  1. Gather the ingredients
  2. Combine the cornmeal, flour sugar, baking powder, salt, and pepper in a medium sized mixing bowl.
  3. Crack the egg into the mixing bowl and then stir the milk in to make a batter.
  4. Heat the oil in a deep fryer or large saucepan to about 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  5. While the oil is heating, pat the hot dogs dry and insert a skewer into each of them.
  6. Dip and roll the skewered hot dogs one at a time into the batter until well coated.
  7. Fry 2 or 3 corn dogs at a time in the preheated oil for about 3 minutes or until lightly browned.
  8. Drain excess oil on paper towels.
  9. Serve your corn dogs with mustard, ketchup, or whatever else you like, and enjoy.
