Pancakes

Who dosen't like pancakes for breakfast. If it's you, keep looking. If you like them though you can try this easy to make pancake recipe.

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup cottage cheese
  • 3/4 cup self-rising flour
  • cooking spray

Directions

  1. Combine the eggs, cottage cheese, and flour in a blender. Then blend until smooth.
  2. Heat a nonstick skillet, griddle, or pan to medium heat. Apply cooking spray.
  3. Place about 3 tablespoons of batter onto the heated surface.
  4. Cook about 1 to 2 minutes or until the edges look sett.
  5. Flip over with a large turner (spatula).
  6. Cook for an addtional 1 to 2 minutes or until the bottom is browned.
  7. Serve with syrup, butter, berries, whatever you like.
